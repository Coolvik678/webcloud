What it is about.   

When a website is not enough: customer portals, booking systems, internal tools, data dashboards. Software that maps exactly how you work, instead of forcing you into the process of an off-the-shelf product.

Some processes do not fit any off-the-shelf program. Then people work with spreadsheets, notes and emails until nobody keeps track any more. Custom software turns this around: it maps exactly how your business works and takes over the steps that repeat every week.

We start at the table, with your process, step by step. From that comes a clickable prototype before a single line of backend exists, so you can see and touch what will be built. It is then built in stages, each of them usable on its own.

Usually this is a web app that runs on desktop, tablet and phone without installation. It runs on its own server, with users and roles, connections to your existing systems and a direct line to me when it should grow.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask me directly.

01How much does custom software cost?

That depends entirely on the process, hence on request. After the analysis you get a fixed price per stage, so the risk stays manageable and every stage is already useful.

02Why not just use off-the-shelf software?

If there is one that fits, I will tell you. Custom work pays off when you would otherwise have to bend to a program’s process or connect several tools by hand.

03Do I need an app in the App Store?

Usually not. A web app runs on every device, can be added to the home screen like an app and needs no approval from a store.

04Who looks after it after launch?

I do. Operation, maintenance and further development are part of it, and you reach the person who wrote the code.
