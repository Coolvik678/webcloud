What it is about.   

A logo is not a picture but a system: mark, type, colour and spacing that together express an attitude. I design brands that work on a business card just as well as on a façade or in an app.

A logo alone does not make a brand. Only together with type, colour and spacing does it become a presence people recognise: on your shop sign, your invoice, your Instagram profile and your website.

It starts with a conversation about who you are, who your customers are and what should last. Then you get three directions, honestly commented, and we develop one of them down to the details.

Finally you receive every file for web and print and a compact brand guide with the rules, so your presence stays right even when someone else designs a brochure. Because brand and website come from one person, both fit together from the start.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask me directly.

01How much does a logo cost?

From €500 for wordmark and symbol with colours and type. With stationery and a fuller brand guide accordingly more; you know the fixed price beforehand.

02How many drafts do I get?

Three directions, each with an honest assessment. We develop one of them together down to the details.

03Which files do I receive?

Every variant of the logo for web and print, in colour, black and white, plus colour values, typefaces and the brand guide.

04Can you refine an existing logo?

Yes. Often the mark is good and only the system around it is missing. Then we keep what people recognise and tidy up the rest.
