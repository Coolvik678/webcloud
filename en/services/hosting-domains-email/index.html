What it is about.   

Domain, mailboxes, certificate, server, backups, updates: everything runs in the background on fast servers in Europe. I set up your addresses so emails arrive and stay out of spam, and move existing domains and mailboxes without downtime. You do not have to worry about a thing. And if something comes up, you reach me, not a hotline.

A website is never quite finished: it needs a server, updates, backups, a valid certificate and email addresses that reliably arrive. I take care of all of that, so you can take care of your business.

Sites run on fast servers in Europe, with daily backups and security updates. I set up your domain and mailboxes so emails do not land in spam: DNS, SPF and DKIM configured properly. Existing domains and mailboxes move over without downtime.

And if something does not work, you call me, not a hotline. Direct support from the best people to help you, engineered with total reliability.

Frequent questions. 

What people often ask before the first meeting. Anything else, ask me directly.

01How much do hosting, domain and email cost?

From €50 a month, including server, certificate, backups, updates and support. How many mailboxes and domains you need, we settle beforehand.

02Can you take over my existing domain and email?

Yes. Domain, mailboxes and old emails move over without your address going down in between.

03Where are the servers?

In Europe. Your data stays in the EU.

04Why do my emails end up in spam?

Usually the records are missing that prove an email really comes from your domain: SPF and DKIM. I set them up properly.

05Who do I reach when something breaks?

Me, directly. No hotline, no ticket gathering dust somewhere.
