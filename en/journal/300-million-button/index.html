The story

UX researcher Jared Spool tells it like this: a large online store asked customers to log in or register before checkout. They replaced the "Register" button with "Continue" and a note that nobody needs an account to buy. 45% more customers completed their purchase, worth an extra $300 million in the first year.

Google once tested 41 shades of blue for its links. According to Dan Cobley, then head of Google UK, the winning shade brought in an extra $200 million a year in ad revenue.

The nine details

  • Size: 52 px tall, comfortably above the 44 px minimum thumb target.
  • Shape: a full pill (border-radius: 999px).
  • Type: a real font, Space Grotesk 500.
  • Color: almost black, #121214.
  • Signal: one red dot with an arrow.
  • Hover: the arrow turns 45° in 300 ms.
  • Light: a soft glow follows your cursor.
  • Magnet: it leans toward you, with a spring.
  • Press: it gives a little, scale .97.
