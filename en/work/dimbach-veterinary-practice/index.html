Veterinary care with heart, on a phone too.

The starting point

People looking for a vet do not want a brochure. They want answers: is the practice open today, what to do in an emergency, how to get an appointment, what other pet owners say.

The solution

A site that works like an app on a phone: navigation at the bottom within thumb reach, calling with one tap, news as notifications behind a bell. Plus services, reviews, frequent questions and directions on the map.

The colours come from the practice itself, a warm rosé instead of clinical blue. The images show the vet with her patients, not people from a stock library.

The result

Live since June 2026. Online appointment requests are prepared and come next.
